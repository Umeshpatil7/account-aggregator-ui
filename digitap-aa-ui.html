Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is an Account Aggregator (AA)?

An Account Aggregator helps you securely share your financial information from banks and other financial institutions with services you choose, only with your consent.

2. Why should I use an Account Aggregator?

It makes sharing financial information faster and easier, without uploading statements or documents manually. You stay in control of what data is shared, with whom, and for how long.

3. Is my data secure?

Yes. Your financial data is shared securely and encrypted. Account Aggregators cannot read, store, or use the financial information being shared.
